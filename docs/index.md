# SAT Math: Foundations to Mastery

A complete SAT Math course. It starts below the SAT — with fractions and
signs — and ends with the hardest questions the second module can throw at
you. You do not have to work through all of it. Most students should not.

## Start with a placement check

The fastest way to waste your preparation time is to study what you already
know. Two checks tell you where to begin.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Level 0 Placement Check](placement/level-0-check.md)**

    15 questions, 20 minutes, no calculator. Tests the arithmetic the SAT
    assumes you already have. Score 12 or more and you skip Level 0 entirely.

- **[Full Diagnostic](placement/full-diagnostic.md)**

    22 questions, 35 minutes — one real module's worth. Tells you which of
    the 48 lessons you actually need.

</div>

## How the course is organised

| Level | What it covers | Share of the test |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| [Level 0](level-0-foundations/index.md) | Foundations — arithmetic, signs, basic algebra | Not tested directly, but underpins everything |
| [Level 1](level-1-algebra/index.md) | Core Algebra | ~35% |
| [Level 2](level-2-data/index.md) | Problem Solving and Data Analysis | ~15% |
| [Level 3](level-3-advanced/index.md) | Advanced Math | ~35% |
| [Level 4](level-4-geometry/index.md) | Geometry and Trigonometry | ~15% |
| [Level 5](level-5-test-craft/index.md) | Test craft — pacing, Desmos, triage | No new maths, real points |

Levels 1 and 3 are about 70% of your score between them. If your time is
short, that is where it should go.

## How to use a lesson

Each lesson is a 45-60 minute unit with the same shape: the idea, three
worked examples, the errors students actually make, and twelve practice
questions with full solutions.

Work the practice set **before** opening the solutions. A question you have
struggled with for two minutes teaches you more than three you read the
answer to. When you get one wrong, write down what you did and what you
should have done — that one-line habit is worth more than any other single
thing in this course.

## What this course does not replace

Official practice tests. Take them in the College Board's Bluebook app,
timed, in one sitting. This course teaches you the mathematics and the
technique; the official tests are how you find out whether it is working.
