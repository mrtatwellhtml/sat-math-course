# Full Diagnostic

This diagnostic is built to feel like one real digital SAT math module, so that
what it tells you about your gaps is worth acting on.

- **22 questions, 35 minutes, one sitting.** Time it honestly.
- **A calculator is allowed throughout**, exactly as on the real test.
- **Do not look anything up.** A question you can only answer with help is a
  question you cannot yet answer, and the point here is to find those.

The questions get harder as you go. If one stalls you, mark it, move on, and
come back. When the timer ends, stop and open the key — it turns your answers
into a study plan.

    ### Build your study plan

    1. Go through the answers table and write down the lesson id beside every
       question you got wrong. That list is your plan — nothing else.
    2. Sort the list by number and start with the lowest one. Later lessons
       assume the earlier ones, so working upward stops you from relearning the
       same idea twice.
    3. Cross a lesson off only after you can do its practice set without
       checking your notes.
    4. Weight your time. Levels 1 and 3 are about $70\%$ of the test, so a gap
       there is worth roughly twice as much as a Level 4 gap. If your list is
       long and your time is short, clear every Level 1 and Level 3 id first,
       then come back for Levels 2 and 4.
    5. If you missed questions 1 through 7 — the questions marked easy — the
       fastest fix is the Level 0 Placement Check, which will tell you whether
       the gap is in the foundations rather than in SAT-specific skills.
