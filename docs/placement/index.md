# Placement

Two instruments, for two different questions.

## Which should you take?

**Take the [Level 0 Placement Check](level-0-check.md) first** if you are
unsure about fractions, negative numbers, or basic equation solving — or if
arithmetic slows you down. It takes 20 minutes and has no calculator.

- **12-15 correct** — skip Level 0 entirely. Go on to the full diagnostic.
- **8-11** — complete only the Level 0 lessons your wrong answers point to.
- **0-7** — work through all of Level 0 before anything else.

**Then take the [Full Diagnostic](full-diagnostic.md).** 22 questions,
35 minutes, calculator allowed — the same shape as one real module. Every
question maps to a specific lesson, so your wrong answers become your
study list.

## Reading your result honestly

The point of a diagnostic is not the score. It is the pattern. When you go
through your wrong answers, sort each one into four boxes:

| Cause | What it means | What fixes it |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| **Content** | You have not properly learned it | Do the lesson |
| **Method** | You knew it, but took a slow or fragile route | Do the lesson's worked examples |
| **Careless** | Sign error, misread, wrong line | A checking habit, not more study |
| **Time** | You could have done it with two more minutes | [Pacing and Triage](../level-5-test-craft/5-3-pacing-and-triage.md) |

These four look identical on a score report and need completely different
responses. A student who is mostly careless does not need more lessons.
