# Level 0 Placement Check

This check tells you one thing: whether you already have the arithmetic and
algebra foundations the rest of the course builds on. It is not a score that
goes anywhere, and nobody is grading you.

- **15 questions, 20 minutes.**
- **No calculator.** Work on paper.
- Answer every question. A guess costs you nothing.

If you get 12 or more right, you skip Level 0 and begin at lesson 1.1. If you
get fewer, the answer key tells you exactly which Level 0 lessons to do — often
two or three, not all eight. Set a timer, work through it in one sitting, then
open the key.

---

    | Score | What to do |

    | 12–15 | Skip Level 0. Start at lesson 1.1. |

    ### What your result means

    A score of 12 or more means the foundations are in place and your time is
    better spent on SAT-specific work — go to 1.1. A score in the middle is the
    most common outcome, and it is useful news: look at every question you
    missed, note the lesson id beside it, and do those lessons only. Two wrong
    answers on the same lesson id is a clear signal; one wrong answer might be a
    slip, so reread the question before deciding. A score of 7 or below means
    the foundations need rebuilding first, and working through Level 0 in order
    will make everything after it faster rather than slower.
